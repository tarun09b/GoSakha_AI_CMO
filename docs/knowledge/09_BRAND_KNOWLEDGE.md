## Flagship product: Sakha
**Tagline:** "Your Hospital's 24/7 AI Assistant."
**One-line description:** An AI-powered patient triage and assistive system — not a receptionist, an intelligent patient assistant that thinks.

### How Sakha works (6 steps, per brochure)
1. Patient calls the hospital's existing number — nothing changes for the patient.
2. Sakha answers instantly in a natural human voice, greeting in the hospital's name; patients cannot tell it is AI.
3. Patient describes their problem in their own words.
4. Sakha asks intelligent follow-up questions.
5. Sakha recommends the right specialist by name and availability.
6. Appointment is booked, a reference number is given, and everything is saved to the hospital's live dashboard instantly.

**Emergency detection:** If Sakha detects critical symptoms (chest pain, stroke signs, breathing difficulty), it immediately tells the patient to call 112 or go to the Emergency Room. Patient safety always comes first — this is a hard-coded behavior, not a marketing claim to soften.

### Feature set (verbatim capability list from brochure)
- 24/7 availability — answers every call at any hour, never misses a patient interaction
- Natural human voice — patients cannot distinguish Sakha from real medical staff
- Unlimited symptom understanding — full AI medical reasoning, not a fixed list
- Automatic doctor routing — matches symptoms to the right specialist and recommends the specific available doctor
- Appointment booking with confirmation reference numbers
- Live hospital dashboard — staff sees all calls, transcripts, and patient summaries in real time from any device
- Doctor management — hospital adds doctors, specializations, availability, fees; Sakha uses this data live
- Unlimited simultaneous calls — no patient ever waits on hold
- Zero setup for hospital staff — GoSakha handles full configuration
