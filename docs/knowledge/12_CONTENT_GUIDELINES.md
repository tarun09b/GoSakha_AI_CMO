# 12 — Content Guidelines

## Voice
Warm, plain-spoken, professional. GoSakha talks to hospital administrators and doctors as peers solving an operational problem — not as marketing targets. Avoid hype adjectives not backed by a verified feature (see `10_HEALTHCARE_KNOWLEDGE.md`).

## Tone by channel
- **Email:** direct, short, one clear ask per message.
- **LinkedIn:** professional, occasionally data/insight-led (e.g. call-volume stats, if sourced).
- **Instagram:** educational and human — patient-safety-forward, never clickbait about medical emergencies.

## What content must always include
- A traceable source fact for every capability claim (cite `09_BRAND_KNOWLEDGE.md` / `10_HEALTHCARE_KNOWLEDGE.md` fact IDs internally, even if not shown to the reader).
- A clear, honest CTA — never manufactured urgency ("only 2 spots left") for a B2B software sale.

## What content must never do
- Invent a clinical outcome, statistic, or certification.
- Imply Sakha replaces a doctor's judgment — it triages and routes, it does not diagnose or treat.
- Use fear-based framing around medical emergencies for marketing purposes.
- Name or disparage a specific competitor without a sourced, verifiable basis.
- Reuse a prospect's or patient's real data in any example/case-study without explicit consent on file.

## Claim-check process (enforced by Content Studio Agent)
1. Draft generated with inline source-fact references.
2. Automated claim-checker cross-references each claim against `10_HEALTHCARE_KNOWLEDGE.md`'s verified set.
3. Any claim without a verified source is flagged, not silently removed — a human reviewer decides to cut or source it.
4. Only claim-checked drafts enter the Approval Queue.

## Versioning & reuse
- Every content asset is versioned; edits create a new version rather than overwriting.
- Approved, high-performing assets are tagged `reusable` and surfaced to agents drafting similar content, reducing redundant generation.

## Accessibility & localization notes
- Keep sentence length short; assume a busy reader skimming on mobile.
- English-first for MVP; template structure should allow future localization without rewrite (no idioms baked into structural copy).
